# PermitAssist Benchmark v1.1 & Permit Manifest Remediation — Independent Architect Review

**TL;DR:** The benchmark's *integrity* is excellent but its *semantics* are misleading: the headline numbers Boban was given are dominated by base-rate artifacts, a scorer/egress contract mismatch, and a truth set that contradicts PermitAssist's own W4 packet doctrine. The engine is much closer to target than 72.8% suggests (its family deficit is a projection bug, its 4 decision misses are all honest abstentions, zero confidently-wrong decisions in 100 cases), while the "Grok for family / Gemini for companions" specialist claims are statistically unsupported — both score *below* trivial constant-answer baselines. The proposed Manifest plan is directionally right but it is largely a restatement of the already-written June doctrine, it assigns model roles the evidence refutes, and it omits the two highest-leverage fixes: repairing the benchmark contract itself and completing companion truth data. Approve with the changes below.

---

## A. Is the benchmark evidence sufficient to support the diagnosis?

**Integrity: verified, real.** I recomputed the arithmetic: every per-dimension pass count in `diagnostic_full_v11_summary.json` sums exactly to the composite (engine 655/900 = 72.8%, engine_luna 637, Grok 602, Gemini 593, Luna 561), and matches the closeout table and quoted facts. Scoreboard rows reconcile with raw envelopes (SHA-bound, atomically saved before parsing, answer bytes unchanged, identity enforcement with fail-closed Grok upstream checks in `benchmark_v11.py:470-488`). The `engine_luna` semantic caveat is confirmed in the raw `path_proof`: `engine_result_supplied_to_model=false`, `model_call_count=1`, `local_merge_customer_path_called=true` — it is Luna-first augmentation merged into the local customer path, not an Engine→Luna finalizer. The plumbing can be trusted.

**Semantics: four defects mean the headline conclusions must not be taken at face value.**

1. **Base-rate saturation makes most cross-path comparisons meaningless.** Truth distribution (verified by grep over `cases.json`): decisions are 95 REQUIRED / 2 CONDITIONAL / 3 NOT_REQUIRED; primary families are 96 BUILDING / 1 ROOFING / 3 NO_PRIMARY_PERMIT; **93 of 100 cases expect zero companions**. Therefore trivial constant baselines score: decision 95%, family 96%, companions 93%. Against these: the engine's 96% decision is +1 over constant-REQUIRED; **Grok's 85% family is 11 points *below* constant-BUILDING**; **Gemini's 69% companions is 24 points *below* constant-empty**. The proposal's model-specialist assignments are built on numbers that don't survive a baseline check.

2. **The benchmark contract is internally incoherent.** Expected labels use families outside the benchmark's own `FAMILIES` enum: companions `DEMOLITION`, `ZONING`, `POOL`, `SIGN`, `MOVING`, `LANDMARKS` and primaries `ROOFING`, `NO_PRIMARY_PERMIT`. The `family()` alias table (`benchmark_v11.py:189-196`) maps `FIRE`→`FIRE_LIFE_SAFETY` but nothing else, so a schema-conformant model **cannot possibly** match 5 of the 7 companion-bearing cases (model ceiling: 95% companions, 96% family). The scorer punishes the answer contract it itself imposes.

3. **The scorer reads fields the engine egress doesn't emit, while the correct typed values sit in the same payload.** Verified in the Anchorage raw envelope: top-level `permit_kind` = "Commercial Building / Tenant Improvement" (display label → scored family fail) while `permits_required[0].family` = `"building"` (correct); scored `companion_permits` items carry no `family`/`status` keys (those live in `related_permits` and `permits_required`); jurisdiction identity exists only nested in `customer_result_summary.jurisdiction` → 4/100 passes, costing the engine ~10.7 composite points (corrected composite ≈ 83.4%, widening its lead). The URL-role dimension is additionally near-circular for the engine (role inferred from the destination it already matched) and adversarial for models (their self-declared role vs. the scorer's heuristic).

4. **Ground truth provenance is the system under test.** 94+ cases carry `truth_origin.type: authenticated_decision_cell`. For the engine this benchmark is a *round-trip self-consistency test* (cell → runtime → egress → scorer); for models it's "reproduce our cells from an evidence packet we hand you" (jurisdiction and sources are literally supplied in the prompt — models' 100% jurisdiction/evidence scores are gimmes). And the cell truth is *heterogeneous in companion depth*: Mat-Su has 6 companions, Albertville 5, but ~60 commercial-TI cases have zero — which directly contradicts the v2.4 W4 doctrine that commercial TI cells must be full filing packets with trades/fire/CO/zoning routes. The engine is being **punished for emitting the enriched package the W4 contract demands** (Anchorage: engine surfaced building+plumbing+mechanical+electrical+zoning REQUIRED, CO conditional; truth said companions=[]).

**What CAN be concluded:** (a) the harness/identity/raw-preservation methodology is sound and reusable; (b) the engine's four decision misses are all honest `VERIFY` abstentions (NYC shed setback, Seattle 500 sq ft threshold, two Raleigh NC statutory exemptions) — **zero confidently-wrong decisions in 500 attempts on the engine path**, a genuinely strong trust posture; (c) the engine drops or relabels typed facts it already owns at egress — cheap, high-yield fix; (d) the runtime emits generic, source-unbacked companion rows (ADA "commonly trigger…", certainty "likely") — a real precision risk and a standing doctrine violation; (e) no model path demonstrated any dimension advantage over trivial baselines.

**What CANNOT be concluded:** true companion precision/recall of anything (truth too thin and shape-confounded); Grok/Gemini specialist value; readiness percentages against reality (vs. against own cells); generalization beyond the alphabetically clustered (A/B-name AHJs), TI-dominated sample.

## B. Root-cause model

Ranked by measured impact on the two target metrics:

1. **Customer projection/egress loss (largest, cheapest).** Family: display label scored instead of the typed `permits_required[0].family` present in the same response → engine 42% instead of ~96%. Jurisdiction: nested, not top-level → 4%. Companions: the scored `companion_permits` shape lacks `family`/`status` keys entirely. This is one contract fix, not a knowledge problem.
2. **Benchmark-ground-truth defects.** Companion truth under-filled on ~90 cases relative to the W4 packet standard; enum non-closure (A.2); base-rate saturation (A.1); truth drawn from the system under test; all-or-nothing companion scoring with no precision/recall decomposition, so "surfaced 5 correct trades truth omitted" scores identically to "returned garbage."
3. **Ontology fragmentation (the proposal is right here).** Three uncoordinated vocabularies: internal `filing_family` lowercase tokens (`building`, `co`, `planning`, `other`), the benchmark `FAMILIES` enum (`OCCUPANCY`, `PLANNING_ZONING`, `FIRE_LIFE_SAFETY`), and cell-truth labels (`ZONING`, `FIRE`, `DEMOLITION`, `POOL`, `SIGN`, `MOVING`, `LANDMARKS`). No canonical mapping exists anywhere.
4. **Generic trigger heuristics in the resolver.** `decision_resolver._kinds_from_text` (line 347) keyword-matches job text and `commercial_default` force-inserts TI kinds; companions get emitted with `certainty: "likely"` and boilerplate "commonly trigger" rationales, no `source_ref`. The Anchorage envelope's own warning admits per-case repair machinery: *"primary permit was repaired or forced away from residential/trade-only leakage."* This is the "keyword classifiers as decision-makers" pattern the June doctrine's stop-doing list already prohibits.
5. **Missing project facts.** NYC/Seattle conditionals require facts (setback distance, measured roof area) the intake never collects; the engine abstains instead of expressing the conditional with its trigger. Only 2 cases here, but the archetype is product-critical.
6. **Data-depth gaps.** NC statutory exemption rules (GS 160D-1110) absent → two honest abstentions where NOT_REQUIRED was provable. Real, narrow, already covered by the doctrine's exemption-evidence rule.
7. **Resolver selection:** no evidence of wrong-cell selection in this run (destination 98%, office 81% — office misses look mostly like authority-string canonicalization variance, correctly deprioritized by the constraints).

## C. Critique of the proposed Permit Manifest architecture

**Right:** No runtime voting; models never author customer truth (points 1, 4-gate, 6). Jurisdiction-first → scope → source-backed trigger rules (point 5). One sealed projection (point 6). No-fix forensic attribution before fixes (point 7). Dev/blind benchmark split (point 8). Canonical family ontology (point 3) — this is the one genuinely *new* and necessary element.

**Not novel — and that matters:** Points 2, 5, 6 are a restatement of the existing June doctrine (`executable-permit-coverage-architecture-and-four-session-delivery.md` specifies `DecisionEnvelope`, per-family verdicts, coverage states, one sealed projection, and a four-session plan; the attribution taxonomy already exists in `ahj-row-count-vs-executable-permit-coverage.md` with **10** categories, a superset of the proposal's 7). The plan should present itself as *executing the standing doctrine*, and use the doctrine's taxonomy, not a new 7-bucket one. Otherwise this becomes yet another parallel decision framework in a codebase that already has too many decision-shaped layers (v2.3.1 cells, v2.4 cells, rule-engine core envelope, `decision_resolver`, `permit_decision` contract, `finalize_customer_public_projection`, `apply_final_customer_egress_contract`).

**Wrong:**
- **Grok-for-family / Gemini-for-companions specialist roles.** Refuted by the data (Section A.1). Offline candidate mining is fine; dimension-locked specialist roles derived from below-baseline scores are cargo cult. Any capable model can propose candidates; the deterministic promotion gate does all the real work.
- **The implied value of `engine_luna`'s 99% decision score.** That +3 over the engine is Luna flipping three honest abstentions into correct answers on a 5-case non-REQUIRED subsample — i.e., model-authored decisions, exactly what constraint 1 forbids at runtime. Kill `engine_luna` as a runtime concept permanently; it may live only as an offline probe.

**Incomplete (the two biggest omissions):**
- **No benchmark-contract repair step.** If the scorer keeps exact-set companion scoring against under-filled truth with a non-closed enum, the *easiest* way to hit 90% companions is to stop emitting enriched companions (39% → 93% overnight) — which would neuter the product and violate the constraints. **The scorer and truth must be fixed before any runtime companion behavior is allowed to change.** Sequencing is a safety property here, and the proposal doesn't state it.
- **No companion-truth/packet-depth data work.** 90% companion recall is unreachable while the truth cells themselves don't record companions. The v2.3.1 depth-enrichment doctrine already specifies the exact schema (per-companion `required|conditional|not_required|unknown` + trigger + authority + `source_ref`). The plan needs a pilot-scoped W4-style completion pass, and it isn't in the eight points.
- Also unaddressed: what happens to the *existing* unsourced keyword-trigger companions (`_kinds_from_text`, `hidden_trigger_detector` — 87 companion references). "Promote no new rule without evidence" is right but silent about demoting the incumbent generic rows.

**Overcomplicated:** A new "immutable DecisionEnvelope" object risks duplicating the sealed-projection machinery that already exists and is tested (`_VerifiedCustomerProjection`, cell locks, `project_customer_response_egress`, the typed `CustomerPermitDecision` dataclass at `server.py:1377` which already has `primary_family` / `required_families` / `conditional_families` / `verify_families`). The win is **consolidation**: make the manifest the single structure the *existing* egress serializes from, and retire the mutator chain behind parity gates — do not add a parallel layer.

## D. Recommended final architecture

Execute the standing doctrine spine, with three additions the doctrine lacks:

1. **One canonical permit-family ontology** (single enum, includes `NO_PRIMARY_PERMIT`; statuses `REQUIRED | CONDITIONAL | NOT_REQUIRED | NEEDS_INPUT | VERIFY`), with three thin adapters: cell-ingest labels → enum; internal `filing_family` → enum; and the benchmark scores **only** enum values. One mapping table, checked in, unit-tested for closure (every label appearing in any cell or truth file must map).
2. **Manifest-as-projection, not manifest-as-new-layer.** Extend `CustomerPermitDecision` into the full manifest (per-family verdict + trigger + authority + `source_ref` + coverage status from the doctrine's six states). The existing trusted egress builds it once; API/UI/report/share all serialize from it; finalizers get wording-only access enforced by the existing forbidden-field / sanitizer-diff machinery. Top-level customer fields gain: canonical `primary_permit_family`, a jurisdiction identity block, and `companion_permits[]` with `family`/`status`/`trigger`/`source_ref`. The benchmark v1.2 scorer reads exactly this contract — product contract and measurement contract converge on one shape.
3. **Companion rows bifurcated by provenance.** Source-backed rows (cell/W4 data with `source_ref`) may carry `REQUIRED`/`CONDITIONAL`. Heuristic keyword-triggered rows may surface only as `VERIFY` leads with visible "confirm with AHJ" framing — or not at all on the REQUIRED surface. This preserves enriched capability (nothing deleted — consistent with the existing `test_residential_address_dependent_companions_demote_to_verify_not_deleted` invariant) while removing the generic-false-positive exposure.

Runtime models: none in the truth path (see H). Offline models: candidate miners and adversarial reviewers feeding the existing staged factory promotion gate.

Feasibility of the targets: with the egress/ontology fix alone, replaying the *same* frozen 100 raws should put family at ~96% (bounded by decision) and jurisdiction at ~100% — that is the Session-2 falsifier. Companions reach ≥90% P/R only within a declared covered scope after truth repair + pilot packet completion; that is exactly what Sessions 1 and 3 exist to deliver.

## E. Bounded implementation plan (four sessions)

**Session 1 — Forensics, ontology, and measurement repair (read-only on runtime).**
Deliverables: (a) per-case attribution of every family/companion/decision miss across the preserved 500 raws using the doctrine's 10-category taxonomy, with the raw-envelope evidence line for each; (b) the canonical ontology + three mapping adapters (spec only); (c) benchmark v1.2 scorer: enum-closed truth, per-companion precision/recall, dangerous-omission class, abstention states, coverage-denominator labels — offline rescore of the same frozen raws, zero provider spend; (d) independent dual-review audit of all 100 expected answers against official sources, especially the 93 empty-companion truths (each becomes `confirmed-none` or `truth-incomplete` with a quote). 
Acceptance gate: 100% of misses attributed with evidence; truth corrections documented with sources; no runtime file touched. Rollback: nothing to roll back. No-go: no code changes, no reruns of providers.

**Session 2 — Egress parity + manifest consolidation, flag-gated.**
Deliverables: manifest projection populated from *existing* internal rows only (no new truth invented); canonical family/jurisdiction/companions surfaced top-level; heuristic companions demoted to VERIFY-leads; no decision-value changes anywhere. Shadow replay of the frozen 100 cases with a byte-diff report on all non-target fields.
Acceptance gates: all existing contract suites green (`test_permit_decision_contract.py`, `test_fix_for_good_customer_boundary_contract.py`, `test_scope_contract_contamination.py`); decision dimension unchanged at 96/100 on replay; family ≥95% and jurisdiction ≥99% on the v1.2 rescore; zero companions *added* by code (only relabeled/demoted); enriched-cell census unchanged. **Falsifier: if family does not reach ≥90% from this fix alone, the Section-B diagnosis is wrong — stop and re-attribute before building anything else.** Rollback: flag off = byte-identical current behavior.

**Session 3 — Pilot packet-depth completion (data, staged only).**
Declare the pilot coverage scope (e.g., commercial TI + residential reroof over a named AHJ set). Complete companion closure per the W4 contract through the existing staged factory discipline (STAGED-ONLY; promotion remains a separate Boban-approved step per standing guardrails — no registry mutation inside this program). Offline model assistance allowed for candidate mining; every promoted row needs official quote + deterministic validation.
Acceptance gates: every staged companion row has `source_ref` or is explicitly `UNKNOWN/VERIFY`; dev-set companion precision and recall ≥90% under the v1.2 scorer within the declared scope. No-go: per-case code patches; any cell promoted without the source gate. Rollback: staging discarded.

**Session 4 — Sealed blind validation, one run.**
A new 100-case blind set built by someone other than the fixer: stratified fresh AHJ sample (not alphabet-clustered), ≥15% non-REQUIRED decisions, companion diversity, threshold conditionals, jurisdiction-ambiguity cases, in-scope and out-of-scope cases labeled; truth from official sources with dual independent review; sealed (hashes committed) before any run; pre-registered metrics; **one run**.
Acceptance: in-scope primary ≥90%, companion precision ≥90% AND recall ≥90%, dangerous omissions = 0, honest abstention on out-of-scope, filing destination/URL-role not degraded vs. v1.1 engine numbers. Rollback point: production never changed during the program; deployment is a separate approved step afterward.

**Anti-loop mechanisms (explicit):** the v1.1 set is diagnosis-only after Session 1 and never re-optimized against; one blind set, one run, pre-registered gates; every fix must name the attribution category it addresses (fixes that enumerate case-specific strings are rejected in review — the repo's existing sediment of `florida_statute_553_502`-style patches is the anti-pattern); the coverage denominator may only change via documented truth corrections, never to dodge a miss; a failed blind run triggers Section J stop analysis, not benchmark v1.3.

## F. Exact metric definitions

- **Coverage denominator:** the published list of declared contracts (`jurisdiction_id × scope-ontology node × fact profile`) frozen before the blind run. Every blind case is pre-labeled in/out of scope by the truth builders, not by the system. **Anti-gaming:** the gate requires both in-scope accuracy ≥90% *and* an in-scope fraction ≥ a pre-registered floor (recommend ≥70% of the blind sample drawn from the declared covered population). Post-seal denominator edits are prohibited; abstentions are never counted in the accuracy numerator.
- **Primary-family accuracy:** exact canonical-enum match per in-scope case; `NO_PRIMARY_PERMIT` is first-class; display strings are not scored.
- **Companion precision:** over in-scope cases, TP/(TP+FP) at (family, status) granularity for emitted statuses in {REQUIRED, CONDITIONAL}; status inflation (truth CONDITIONAL → emitted REQUIRED) is FP. VERIFY/NEEDS_INPUT emissions are excluded from precision but budget-capped (below).
- **Companion recall:** reported as two numbers — recall over truth-REQUIRED families (must be emitted REQUIRED) and recall over truth-CONDITIONAL families (must be emitted CONDITIONAL, or REQUIRED only when collected facts resolve the trigger).
- **Complete-set exact match:** all-families-all-statuses per case; secondary headline metric (target ≥75%), not a gate, so single-status noise can't fail the program while still being visible.
- **Dangerous omission rate:** fraction of in-scope cases where a truth-REQUIRED family (primary or companion) is absent from the output with *no* VERIFY/NEEDS_INPUT placeholder for that family. Gate: **zero**. This is the customer-safety number; it is also why demote-not-delete is mandatory.
- **Abstention/needs-input handling:** NEEDS_INPUT is scored correct iff the truth marks the case fact-dependent and the asked fact is the trigger fact; a per-case abstention budget (e.g., ≤2 VERIFY families among in-scope emissions) prevents "VERIFY everything" gaming; a confident answer on an out-of-scope case counts as a dangerous failure, an abstention there counts as correct-out-of-scope.
- **Decision non-regression:** blind in-scope decision ≥95% with zero REQUIRED↔NOT_REQUIRED confident flips; dev replay stays at 96/100 or improves only via cell/rule evidence, never via heuristic defaults.

## G. Tests/invariants required

Keep green throughout: the three existing suites (decision contract incl. threshold-conditional preservation and positive-exemption-evidence NOT_REQUIRED; customer-boundary incl. v2.4 cell-lock preservation and companions-demote-not-delete; scope-contract contamination/firebreak suite). Add:

1. **Egress parity invariants:** canonical `primary_permit_family` equals the enum-mapped family of the primary `permits_required` row on every 200 response; jurisdiction identity block always present; every companion row carries family+status, and REQUIRED/CONDITIONAL rows carry `source_ref` — enforced at projection time, fail-closed to VERIFY.
2. **No-unsourced-authority invariant:** projection-layer assertion that no REQUIRED companion exists without provenance (this codifies the v2.3.1 "no source_ref → no customer factual claim" rule in code, not review).
3. **Enriched-capability census:** fixture replay of N enriched AHJs asserting W4 packet fields (companions, fees, portal, CO routes) still render — the explicit anti-neutering anchor; cell counts per state/track unchanged.
4. **Trust-boundary non-regression:** existing forbidden-field scrub, sealed-projection tamper fail-closed, cell-lock precedence, and decision-mutation prohibition tests unchanged; a new test that the manifest path cannot alter `permit_decision` relative to the resolver output.
5. **Benchmark self-tests:** enum-closure CI check on any truth file (every label maps into the ontology); scoreboard reproducibility (offline rescore of committed raws reproduces the committed scoreboard byte-for-byte); baseline report (constant-answer baselines printed next to every path so base-rate artifacts can never hide again).
6. **Filing destination/URL non-regression:** destination_exact and url_role dimensions ≥ current engine values on dev replay.

## H. Model-role recommendation

- **Deterministic engine: the only runtime authority.** All customer decisions, families, companions, statuses, and filing routes come from authenticated cells + executable rules. This is both the constraint and what the evidence supports — the engine path was the only one with zero confidently-wrong decisions.
- **Runtime LLMs: wording only,** inside the sealed manifest, under the existing sanitizer-diff/forbidden-scope machinery. No model may flip, add, or remove a regulated field. **`engine_luna` must be retired as a runtime architecture**: its 99% decision score is three model-authored abstention flips — the exact failure mode the constraints prohibit, looking like a win.
- **Grok / Gemini: offline candidate miners and adversarial reviewers only, without dimension-locked specialties.** The benchmark shows Grok below the constant-BUILDING baseline on family and Gemini below the constant-empty baseline on companions; there is no evidence for the proposed specialist assignment. Use whichever models are cheap/available to *propose* candidate rules, exemptions, and companion routes, and to red-team staged packets; nothing they produce is promoted without an official-source quote plus deterministic validation plus the staged-factory gate.
- **Luna: no distinct role** beyond the same offline pool.

## I. Product-input recommendation

Yes — targeted follow-up questions are required, and they are the correct resolution of the NYC/Seattle archetype, but bounded: a question may be asked only when an in-scope, source-backed trigger predicate references a fact the intake didn't provide (setback distance, roof area vs. a cited threshold, occupancy/use change, trade scopes present); maximum ~5, each displaying its trigger source. Unresolved conditionals must render as `CONDITIONAL` with both branches visible ("Permit required if the repair area exceeds 500 sq ft — Seattle SDCI") rather than either a silent omission or a bare VERIFY. Unresolved conditional *companions* stay listed with status CONDITIONAL/NEEDS_INPUT and their trigger text — never dropped (the existing demote-not-delete test is the right pattern). Questions must never block the primary answer; they refine it.

## J. Top risks and stop conditions

1. **Scorer-first sequencing is violated.** If anyone "improves" companion scores before the v1.2 scorer + truth audit land, the cheapest path is suppressing enriched companions (39%→93%) — product neutering disguised as a win. Stop condition: any diff that reduces emitted source-backed companions before Session 1 completes is rejected outright.
2. **Truth repair contaminates measurement.** If the people fixing the runtime also author the corrected truth, the blind run is theater. Mitigation: dual independent review with quotes. Stop condition: blind-set companion metrics diverge >15 points from the repaired dev set — treat as truth-gaming evidence, halt and audit the truth process, not the engine.
3. **The projection diagnosis is wrong.** If Session 2's egress parity fix does not lift dev-replay family accuracy to ≥90%, the loss is not where this review located it — stop, re-attribute, do not proceed to data work on a false premise.
4. **Companion truth is genuinely unknowable at scale.** If Session 3's source gate fails on >20% of pilot cells because official sources simply don't enumerate companion routes, then ≥90% companion *recall of REQUIRED statuses* is the wrong target — the honest architecture answer is CONDITIONAL/VERIFY companions with triggers, and the program should stop and renegotiate the metric rather than loop, since no engineering fixes absent law.
5. **Blind-run failure with novel causes.** If >30% of blind-run misses attribute to categories that never appeared in Session 1's forensics, the architecture hypothesis itself is falsified — stop; do not schedule benchmark v1.3 against the same design.
6. **Scope creep into a fifth decision layer.** If the manifest lands as an additional structure while `decision_resolver` heuristics, late mutators, and "repaired or forced" warnings survive underneath it, nothing was consolidated. Gate: Session 2 must show the mutator chain shrinking (fewer regulated-field writers), not growing.

## K. Verdict

The Manifest direction is correct and largely already doctrine; the plan is approvable only with these changes: benchmark contract and truth repair as the mandatory first phase; ontology unification with enum-closure enforcement; egress parity as the first and falsifying code change; demotion of unsourced keyword-trigger companions; deletion of `engine_luna` as a runtime concept; removal of the Grok/Gemini dimension-specialist roles in favor of gate-guarded offline mining; the anti-gaming coverage-denominator rules; and the explicit stop conditions above.
