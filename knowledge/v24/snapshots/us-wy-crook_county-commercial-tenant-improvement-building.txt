To Whom It May Concern: Crook County, Wyoming, does not have Land Use Regulations, Zoning Regulations, Adopted Building Codes, or Building Permit Requirements; does not do Residential, Commercial, or Industrial building construction inspections; and does not issue Certificates of Occupancy. Please do not contact us concerning this, as we have no additional information to provide. Also ; Crook C ounty, Wyoming, recommends that all building construction comply with the codes and referenced standards adopted by the State of Wyoming, which are as follows : 2024 International Fire Code, 2024 International Building Code, 2024 International Fuel & Gas Code, 2024 International Mechanical Code, 2024 International Existing Building Code and the 2023 edition of the National Electrical Code . If you need more information concerning these codes and referenced standards, please visit: https://wsfm.wyo.gov/. Tim othy R. Lyons Director P.O.
